What this app does with your child's information

Nothing leaves your device. Your child's name, their answers, their progress and their results are created on this device and stay on it. We never receive them and could not see them if we wanted to.

There is no account and no server. Progress is saved on this device so your child can carry on where they left off. Nothing is ever uploaded, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time, or save a copy to a file to move it to another device.

We collect no email, password, date of birth, address or photograph. There is no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

A nickname works perfectly well. You do not have to use your child's real name.

The AI tutor is switched off. One optional feature, the Ask tab, sends a typed question over the internet to be answered. It is off until an adult switches it on, and even then it sends only the question and the school year — never your child's name.

Deleting is instant. Removing a player deletes their record straight away, and there is a button to erase everything on this device.

The full privacy policy is supplied with the app. If anything here does not match what you were given, do not continue.
